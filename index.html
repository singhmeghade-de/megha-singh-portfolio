Case One · AI in Marketing · Infineon Automotive

Multi-agent GenAI workflow for marketing collateral

“Product decks were bought from an external agency at four figures each. I built the internal alternative and quantified it.”

€1,100 to €1,400

Challenge

High-quality product decks depended on external agency production: slow, expensive, and unable to keep pace with content demand across five business divisions.

Approach

Ran a documented comparison of Jasper.ai against ChatGPT on five defined criteria, then sequenced multiple AI agents for drafting, structuring and formatting, pre-filled from product data exports, with output mapped onto the approved brand template.

Result

Same-day internal turnaround replacing agency lead times, a defensible basis for the department’s tooling decisions, and roughly €430K of agency spend addressed across a 355-document update programme.

Case Three · AI Automation · Infineon Automotive

Jira triage automation in n8n

“The team was hand-sorting every incoming marketing request. I replaced that with a workflow.”

2,389
tickets processed, 8 months live
8h → <1h
request-to-owner notification
~330h
team capacity returned per year
Challenge

Marketing requests arrived in Jira with no routing logic. Tickets sat unsorted, and assignment depended on one person remembering who owned what.

Approach

Built three parallel n8n branches running hourly: product-line extraction from free-text descriptions applied as labels, a routing model mapping 15+ product lines to four owners, and Webex status alerts with unassigned requests flagged for review.

Result

4.5 to 7 minutes of manual handling eliminated per request. Routing accuracy no longer depends on memory, the system scales through launch and campaign volume surges at no extra effort, and every action is logged into an audit trail that didn’t exist before.

Case Four · Content Strategy · Infineon Automotive

Content governance from an invisible backlog

“Nobody knew how stale the marketing library had become. I made it measurable, then made it a programme.”

418
outdated assets identified
355
updates scoped into a governed queue
€70K
programme budget managed
Challenge

No one could say how much of the content library was out of date, or which pieces actually mattered to prioritise.

Approach

Cross-referenced document age against download volume: older than four years, more than 25 downloads, segmented by type (235 product decks, 109 app notes, 74 application decks), and cross-checked against OKR priorities.

Result

Backlog converted into a tracked Trello queue, agency scope and quotations managed across two delivery batches, run inside a €70K budget with quarterly reporting made repeatable.

Challenge

Organic Tattva (zero awareness, premium organic positioning) and McVitie’s (strong national equity, zero regional presence) both needed a first-ever distributor built from scratch across two Indian states, across three channels at once.

Approach

Tiered retailer incentive and margin schemes to overcome stocking reluctance, 15 to 30 in-store activations across general trade, modern trade and e-commerce, outlet-level demand forecasting via a proprietary app, and renegotiated credit terms to accelerate cash rotation.

Result

Consistent monthly sales targets hit for both brands within three months, 37% growth in distributor sales across the regional network, and high distributor retention through loyalty incentive design.
